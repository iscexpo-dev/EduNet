# EduNet Pilot Plan

## Objectives
Validate reliable learning workflows, role isolation, accessibility, low-bandwidth behavior, teacher grading, and parent progress visibility using synthetic accounts before any real student data is introduced.

## Participants and onboarding
Use authorized administrators, teachers, parents, and students. Provide role-specific training, synthetic data instructions, support contacts, privacy expectations, and an incident escalation path. Do not invite real schools or users until the release owner approves the environment and consent process.

## Scenarios and KPIs
Measure activation, successful login, course enrollment, lesson engagement, assignment and assessment completion, teacher grading activity, parent engagement, uptime, API error rate, latency, accessibility defects, low-bandwidth completion, and support requests. Collect structured feedback after each scenario and record incidents without unnecessary student identifiers.

## Success and stop conditions
Success requires agreed targets for login, enrollment, completion, reliability, support response, accessibility, and zero unresolved critical authorization or privacy issues. Stop the pilot for a suspected data exposure, authentication bypass, material data loss, sustained outage, or inability to recover safely. The release owner records a go/no-go decision with evidence.
